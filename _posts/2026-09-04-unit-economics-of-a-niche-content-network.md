I run a small network of niche sites that focus on practical how‑to content. The goal is modest: reach a $30k per month run‑rate within two years. I thought it would be useful to share the numbers that keep the plan realistic.

**Traffic and revenue**
Each site targets a long‑tail keyword set that brings about 150 k pageviews per month at an average CPM of $4. That translates to $600 in display revenue per site. The real lift comes from affiliate links – I see a 1.2 % conversion rate on product pages and an average order value of $70. With a 5 % commission that yields roughly $500 per month per site.

**Cost structure**
Content creation is the biggest expense. I write most articles myself at a rate of $0.05 per word; a 1 200‑word guide costs $60. Publishing three new pieces per week per site adds $720 monthly. Hosting on a low‑cost VPS is $30 per month per site, and the remaining $150 covers tools, SEO services, and a small buffer.

**Break‑even math**
Revenue per site: $1 100. Cost per site: $900. That leaves $200 profit per site per month. Scaling to 15 sites gets us to $3 000 profit per month, and with incremental traffic growth and a few higher‑ticket affiliate programs we can push the total to the $30k target by month 24.
