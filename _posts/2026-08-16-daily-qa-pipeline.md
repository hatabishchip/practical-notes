I've been working on a daily QA pipeline for the content-site network I run. The goal is to score every article on depth, structure and anti-AI patterns. The pipeline uses a combination of natural language processing and machine learning algorithms to evaluate each article. It then auto-regenerates the ones that fail to meet the standards. For example, on [qepam.com](https://qepam.com) I've seen a significant improvement in the quality of personal finance guides after implementing this pipeline. I also take inspiration from the weekly editorial awards for AI tools on [aceju.com](https://aceju.com) to refine my anti-AI pattern detection. The pipeline is still a work in progress, but it's been a valuable tool in maintaining the quality of content across the network. If you're interested in the technical details of how it works, I'm happy to answer any questions.
